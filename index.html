A home bakery with three years of cakes and counting

K.bakezz began three years ago in my own kitchen. I love the quiet part of baking most: piping a border one shell at a time, placing the last tiny flower, writing a name in soft white script.

Today I bake vintage-style buttercream cakes, floral designs, bridal cakes, birthday cakes and a chocolate layer cake with caramel filling that people keep asking for. Every order is made for one person and one occasion.

I accept orders anytime and bake for customers across the UAE, subject to delivery availability. Send me a message and tell me what you are celebrating.

  • Elegant vintage-style buttercream with delicate hand piping
  • Floral decorations piped in buttercream, never plastic toppers
  • Personalised messages and designs for each occasion
  • Chocolate layer cakes with a smooth caramel filling
  • Individually packaged cake slices for gifting
